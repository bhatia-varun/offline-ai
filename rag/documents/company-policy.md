# Example Company Flexible Work and Leave Policy

## Working from another location

Employees may work from another domestic location for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Manager approval must be obtained at least five working days before travel. Employees must remain available during their team's core collaboration hours and must use the company VPN.

International remote work requires approval from the employee's manager, People Operations, Security, and Tax. Requests should be submitted at least 30 days in advance. Approval is not guaranteed.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Requests for one or two days should normally be made at least three working days in advance. Longer leave should normally be requested at least two weeks in advance.

## Security

Confidential customer information must not be downloaded to personal devices. Company-managed devices, disk encryption, multi-factor authentication, and the VPN are required when working outside a company office.
